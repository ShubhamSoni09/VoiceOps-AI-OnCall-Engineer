# Product

## Register

product

## Users

VoiceOps is for engineering teams working in incident response and collaborative coding sessions. Users are on-call engineers, reviewers, tech leads, and teammates who move in and out of a shared workspace while an AI agent stays online.

## Product Purpose

VoiceOps should behave like a persistent AI teammate. It listens to team discussion, answers code questions, performs approved repository actions, records what changed, and gives returning teammates a concise handoff of what happened while they were away.

Success means a teammate can join a session and quickly understand who said what, what the agent did, which files changed, which tests ran, what decisions were made, and what still needs human review.

## Brand Personality

Precise, calm, accountable.

VoiceOps should feel like a senior on-call teammate: fast enough for incident work, quiet enough for focus, and explicit about actions and uncertainty.

## Anti-references

Avoid marketing-page theatrics, oversized hero composition, decorative AI gradients, vague assistant chatter, silent code edits, fake certainty about speaker identity, and UI that hides audit trails behind decorative cards.

## Design Principles

1. Treat the agent as a participant, not a floating chatbot.
2. Every code action must answer who requested it, what changed, what ran, and what remains open.
3. Handoffs should summarize meaningful changes since the teammate was last present.
4. Collaboration history should become structured project memory, not an endless transcript.
5. Speaker identity should be useful but honest about uncertainty.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast for product surfaces. Preserve keyboard access, visible focus states, reduced-motion behavior, and readable dense information. Voice identity and diarization features must expose confidence and correction paths instead of assuming perfect recognition.
